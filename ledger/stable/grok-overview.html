Welcome to Grok

View as Markdown
Meet grok-4.7

Grok is SpaceXSI's assistant, available on the web at grok.com and in the iOS and Android apps. Sign in once and your conversations, settings, and subscription stay in sync across every platform.

What you can do

  • Chat — ask questions, brainstorm, write, and work through problems in a natural back-and-forth.
  • Create images and video with Grok Imagine.
  • Talk to Grok hands-free with voice.
  • Upload files — PDFs, images, spreadsheets, code, audio, and more — for analysis, extraction, and summarization.
  • Connect your tools so Grok can reach your email, files, and calendar right inside a chat. See Connectors.

Plans and usage

Grok is free to start. Paid SuperGrok plans raise your limits and unlock more across every product, drawing from a single weekly usage allowance you can spend however you like. For how limits, Extra Usage Credits, and billing work, see the FAQ.

Where to go next

  • Help & FAQ — billing and subscriptions, accounts, image and video generation, file uploads, and weekly usage limits.
  • Connectors — link Grok to the apps and data you already use.
  • Business & Enterprise — team workspaces, licenses, and organization controls.
  • Grok Bot — hand real work to AI teammates on a persistent cloud computer.
